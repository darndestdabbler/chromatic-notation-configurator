The dozenal clef's digits
=========================

The preview draws the engine's dozenal clef with the outlines of the digits
'5' and '0' from TeX Gyre Schola Bold (texgyreschola-bold.otf, as shipped
with LilyPond 2.24), in cnb/src/cnb-clef.js (DOZENAL_DIGITS). The outlines
are copied unaltered, in the font's own units.

TeX Gyre Schola is based on URW++ Century Schoolbook L, as is C059, the
bold serif LilyPond 2.24 engraves the dozenal clef in; the two fonts' '5'
and '0' have the same metrics and outlines.
