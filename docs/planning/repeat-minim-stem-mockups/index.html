A repeated minim's stem on its notehead

Session 72, for the operator's pick (the session-69 try-out, docs/uat-status.md, rough edge 1). Drawn with the Configurator's own cards in repeat mode at the default overlap (1/3), on C (stem up) and A (stem down). Today a minim's stem is placed at the anchor's share of the whole group of heads, so a centred stem stands in the notch between the two heads. The rule, which engine 1.9.0 engraves with cnb:multinote-stem-on-head: the stem attaches to one head of the group as a single head's stem does, the last head when the stem is up and the first when it is down. A centred stem follows the same rule, standing on the chosen head's centre.

Kite 4-369's minim anchors were set for placement across the group (0.73 semitones off the head's centre, toward the other head), so it is drawn with its own minim anchors and with its crotchet anchors, which are centred.
