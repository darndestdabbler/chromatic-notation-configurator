Key signature editor mock-ups

Session 53, for review before the amendment to key-signature-designer-design.md (§13) is approved. Every staff, notehead, ledger and Emmentaler sign is drawn with the Configurator's own code; the marks come from a prototype of the amendment's one model. Tails are drawn as strokes here; the build imports them as shapes.

1. The editor (Clairnote SN in B♭ major, the flats selected)

The Key signature section, its panel below the staff's canvas. The magnified signature is the picked key's. A click on a mark selects its category, here every flat: the solid outline is the mark clicked, with its reference point (the cross, on its pitch) and the resize handle; the dashed outlines are the other flats the edit moves with it. Dragging the mark sets the flats' offset, and the handle their size, in semitones. Beneath, the mode's 15 keys, so an edit can be seen in every key.

2. At concert size

Each signature in a sharp key and a flat key, first at concert size (LilyPond's 20-point staff at the engine's 7/4, one staff space 11.7 px, as the screen shows it at 100%), then three times as large. The whole engraved staff is drawn: on the default 4-8 staff, two line sets either side of middle C.
