The Clef steps, and dropdowns

Session 78, for the operator's approval (the session-77 try-out, docs/uat-status.md items 4–6; the design is docs/planning/clef-steps-design.md). The previews are drawn with the Configurator's own card collection, at the canvas's Large size (16 px a semitone), with the pitch names to the left of the staff. The controls here work: change the clef, its anchor, where it sits and the space after it, or drag the clef up and down and its corner to resize it, and the preview follows. The red ringed cross is the clef's anchor, and the pitch it sits on is highlighted in the names.
