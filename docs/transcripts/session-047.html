Session 47: Designing the key-signature designer

Why. A signature should be as easy to design as the noteheads, and reach Clairnote's, traditional ones and the scale stack alike (operator, 2026-11-08), without a designer defining every key in every mode (operator, session 42).

  1. Register.
  2. A design document only, no code, from the operator's outline in docs/uat-status.md (session 42): two kinds of signature, traditional accidentals or scale notes.   3. The design goes to a cross-provider consult (GPT and Gemini) before the operator approves it, and it plans the sessions that build it.
