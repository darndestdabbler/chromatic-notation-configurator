Session 39: The workflow in the order a notation is designed

Why. In the session-38 rebuild the operator judged a notehead's height against the staff before they had set either, and asked for the sections in the order a designer settles them, each step resting on the last (operator, 2026-11-09).

  1. Register.
  2. The page starts, as now, from a conventional notehead on the 4-8 staff. The first step sets the general notehead height, for every notehead, on that staff, and the notehead drawn is that height.
  3. The sections follow in this order: notehead height, Staff, Ledger lines, Noteheads (the shape, colour, fill and size of selected noteheads), Key signature, Clef. Pitch stays beside Staff, and Advanced and Export stay last.
