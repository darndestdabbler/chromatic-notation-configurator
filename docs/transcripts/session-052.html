Session 52: Sharps and flats sized to the noteheads

Why. Session 51 draws the signs at LilyPond's size, 3 staff spaces tall for a sharp. On the default staff, at 4 semitones a staff space, that is 12 semitones, an octave, far too big beside its noteheads (operator, 2026-11-11). The signs are to be sized in semitones, as the noteheads are, and the size is to be adjustable.

  1. Register.
  2. The size. keys.keySignature.signs.heightSemis: the sharp's height, measured from the top left of its upper bar to the top left of its lower bar, in semitones (axis units under a pitch axis, as a notehead's height). null, the default, is automatic: the least of the noteheads' mean height (the twelve pitch classes' crotchet noteheads as they resolve with no key), one staff space, and 3 semitones (operator, 2026-11-11, after seeing 2 and 3 semitones on the default staff). Each part covers one kind of staff. The 3-semitone cap holds back very large noteheads, such as Kite 4-369's 4 semitones, which would otherwise make enormous signs. The noteheads set the size on most staffs. A staff space sets it on very dense staffs (0-5-7 and Avique, a line every semitone), where it gives LilyPond's own proportion, the bars a staff space apart. The rule gives 2.72 on the default staff, 3 on Kite, 1 on 0-5-7 and Avique, and 2 on the rest of the gallery. A number set by hand stays as it is when the noteheads or the staff change. The flat is scaled by the same factor as the sharp. The factor is the size ÷ the sharp's bar spacing (about 1.05 staff spaces; measure it from the outline in each optical size) ÷ the semitones per staff space. The columns are the scaled signs' widths apart. Validation: null, or a finite number greater than 0, with a warning outside 1–12 semitones.
