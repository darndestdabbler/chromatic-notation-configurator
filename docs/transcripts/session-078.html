Session 78: Designing the Clef steps, and dropdowns

Why. The operator's try-out (session 77): the Clef steps are more complicated than they need to be. "Choose a clef" offers the built-in clefs as buttons and "Clefs" lists them again with the imported ones; the preview sits above the options rather than beside them; nothing on the canvas marks the clef's anchor, and the highlighted bar at its bottom edge (the resize handle) reads as one. And the page uses rows of buttons and radio buttons where a dropdown would be more economical, the key-signature designer most of all.

  1. Register.
  2. A design document in docs/planning/ with mock-ups the operator looks at before approving it, for the Clef steps (10 and 11):
  • one list of clefs, built-in and imported, with Import, in place of "Choose a clef" and "Clefs";
  • the options on the left and the preview on the right, larger than today's, with the pitch names to the left of the staff as the canvas draws them, and changing as each option changes: the anchor marked, and the clef moved and resized live;
  • the options in order: a. the clef, chosen or imported; b. its anchor, top, middle, bottom or a point picked on the glyph; c. where the anchor sits on the staff, one of the pitches or a semitone of the designer's own, also by dragging; d. the space after the clef (none is needed before it: the engine pads the right only, and the preview follows);
  • what the two steps become, and what the export writes for each choice (LY spec §5.5, §5.5a), unchanged where it can be.
  1. In the same document, the page's rows of buttons and radio buttons that would be more economical as dropdowns, each with its mock-up: the key-signature designer's Stacks first (operator), then its other rows, and the other steps' choices. A choice whose options are pictures, or a one-at-a-time choice the designer flips while watching the canvas, may stay as buttons; the document says which and why.

*(Done in session 78, 2026-11-18: the design, [docs/planning/clef-steps-design.md](../planning/clef-steps-design.md), approved by the operator that day after looking at the mock-ups, [docs/planning/clef-steps-mockups/](../planning/clef-steps-mockups/). The operator's decisions (design §5): the Clef steps stay two, 10 (the clef, its anchor, its height) and 11 (where it sits); no setting for the space after the clef, since an engine measurement showed the engraved clef 0.75 staff spaces after the staff's start and its gutter moving nothing after it; the export unchanged.)*
