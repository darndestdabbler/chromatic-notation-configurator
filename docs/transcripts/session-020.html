Session 20: Design revision: the canvas reflows instead of scaling

Why. Reviewing the session 19 design on 2026-11-06, the operator asked that the staff canvas never scroll sideways to show the octave, and that the noteheads not change size as the window is resized. Session 19's §6 did the opposite: it scaled display.pxPerSemi to fit the width. The operator proposed a table of 13 (or 14) columns, with the staff cut into vertical slices centred in each column, so the spacing between notes adjusts on its own, and asked that it look better. The preview already draws each column's noteheads, stems and ledgers in its own SVG. Only the staff lines share one background SVG, sized to the columns' fixed total, so the change is to the column widths and the staff background, not a new renderer. This session revises the design and its mock-ups. It changes no application code.

  1. Register.
  2. Revise docs/planning/notehead-first-editor-design.md (§6, §7, and wherever else it touches):
  • Fixed size. Noteheads are drawn at a fixed px per semitone, whatever the window width. Choose the editor's default and say why.
  • A table of columns. 13 equal pitch columns, each at least as wide as the widest column the config needs, sharing any spare width equally, with each column's drawing centred. The label strip and the clef and key-signature gutters form a fixed 14th column on the left.
  • The staff lines. Weigh one staff SVG per row, stretched to the row's width (lines drawn to 100%), against per-column staff slices, including the seam where two slices meet at a fractional pixel. Pick one and say why.
  • One row or two. When one row does not fit, the canvas shows two: C to F♯ and G to C′, each starting with its own label strip and gutters. Page code picks the row count from the space available. The rendering modules stay pure: the collection takes a rows option and reports its minimum width. Say what happens below the two-row minimum.
  • Looks better. Equal columns, plain pitch labels instead of boxed chips, full-height selection and hover marks, a quiet gutter for the label strip, and space between the two rows, each judged in screenshots.
  • Renumber the proposed program for this session's insertion (sessions 23–30, release last). Update its session 24 equivalent (the new page) to build this canvas, and say which cnb/src modules change (at least cnb-collection.js) and how the preview baseline is kept.

Judge them against the design, revise until they hold up, and replace the screenshots in the design's §10. Remove the mock-up stylesheet's unused side-panel styles.
