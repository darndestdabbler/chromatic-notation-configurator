Rotated stem rects

In cnb/ui/svg-importer.js, read a stem rect's anchor from its transformed edges, not from the bounding box of the transformed rect. Map the rect's local top-centre and bottom-centre through its transform; an up rect's anchor is the one lower on the page (greater y) and a down rect's the one higher, and when the two are level (a quarter turn) the local bottom-centre for an up rect and the local top-centre for a down rect. So rotate(90) on {0, 0, 10, 20} anchors an up rect at (-20, 5), and Inkscape's scale(-1) flip still reads the down anchor at (60, -90). Keep transformRect's contract for the box and its existing tests; update the doc comments. Tests in cnb/tests/cnb-svg-importer-tests.js: the rotate(90) up anchor through extract and buildShape, a rotated down rect, and the M8 flip test unchanged.
