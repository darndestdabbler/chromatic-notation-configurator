Session 24: Pitch, nudges and staff spacing

Why. Pitch (the middle of the staff) and the per-pitch-class nudge are the editor's two pitch variables (design §3, §4). This session builds their arithmetic with no UI.

  1. Register.
  2. cnb/ui/pitch-model.js, pure functions: moving the pitch by *d* semitones, rewriting the midpoint, staff pattern, ledger templates and line overrides together (§3.1); nudges to and from pitchAxis (stride 12, fractional positions, axisUnitsPerStaffSpace = the staff spacing; the section removed when every nudge is 0) with each nudge's limits (§3.2); converting a stride-7 map to nudges, and the ±1 test that keeps a map that cannot convert (§3.3, §3.5); renaming a notehead, which rotates the look table and the nudges (§4.1, §4.3).
  3. The staff spacing accepts a decimal (§3.4). An engine render of Twinline in nudge form (staff spacing 3.43) confirms it engraves at the positions of the stride-7 fixture, through cnb/tests/engine-probe.js or the round-trip recipe, skipping with a reason when lilypond is not on PATH.
  4. Tests in cnb/tests/cnb-pitch-model-tests.js, including Twinline's nudges from the §3.3 table and the no-reordering limits.
