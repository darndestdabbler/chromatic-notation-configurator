It implements design section 4: the stacks assigned over the whole scale and its upper tonic (degrees: even index left, odd right; parity: odd semitones left, even right; parity-stacks: a new stack to the right at each change of the semitone's parity, the tonic's first; parity-zigzag: the other stack at each change), then mirrored for left-in-sharp-keys in a key with sharps (not under parity), then the notes left out (the upper tonic when off; with tonic-and-half-steps all but the tonic and both notes of each half step); each mark's look is the notehead resolved at crotchet with the key's name, then looks.all, then looks.flat or looks.sharp by its spelling, then alternate on every second stack counted from the tonic's; the place is the middle (today's tonicSemi, tonicOctave still overriding it), or the bottom, top or every line set (runs of the engraved staff's lines one staff space apart, midpoint - 12 to midpoint + 12, by CNBStaff.lilypondPosition), the tonic's octave whose middle is nearest the set's middle, the higher on a tie, one copy per set, and two marks at one semitone in one stack drawn once; the columns are the widest mark's width apart, in staff spaces; a bar between each pair of neighbouring stacks, spanning midpoint +- extentSemis in the middle or its copy's marks on a line set, its thickness thicknessPct (in staff spaces) divided by the number of bars when shareThickness is on; overlaps are two marks in one stack whose distance on the grid is less than half the sum of their resolved heights. measure, padding, gutterWidth and render draw from marks, keeping today's px arithmetic for two columns and the pitch axis's offsets, so the signature baseline and preview-baseline.json still match byte for byte.
