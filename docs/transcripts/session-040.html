Session 40: Stems where the notehead's SVG puts them

Why. Since session 33 a stem's edge sits on its anchor, as LilyPond's does, so a stem drawn centred in the notehead's SVG (Kite 4-369, 45deg-369) previews and engraves half a stem off centre. Session 33 left this to the operator, who chose: a stem goes where the SVG's stem rect puts it, and the default notehead's stems take the conventional positions, up on the right and down on the left (operator, 2026-11-09).

  1. Register.
  2. A stem rect in an imported SVG places the stem itself, in the preview and in the engraving: the Kite and 45deg stems are centred.
  3. The default notehead's up stem is on its right and its down stem on its left, in the preview and the engraving. The operator may supply the positions.
  4. The fixtures and export baselines follow, with an engine round trip, and the LY spec records the change.

Work Plan
