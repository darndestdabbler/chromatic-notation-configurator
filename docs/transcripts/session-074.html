Session Plan

Session 74: Designing how noteheads show duration

Why. The operator's try-out (session 73, rough edge 1): the notehead panel edits one look a pitch class for every duration, the duration buttons above the canvas only choose the rows drawn, and with one duration shown a designer expects to edit that one. The operator's model (2026-11-16), with very few options:

  • A. Conventional noteheads: the conventional crotchet, minim, semibreve and breve; only the colour varies by pitch; the designer sets the notehead's height.
  • B. A custom notehead, its shape, colour and fill varying by pitch, and duration shown either way:
  • i. Hollows, crotchets always solid: a minim one hollow head with a stem, a semibreve one hollow head without, a breve a double notehead or two tied semibreves, as the designer chooses.
  • ii. Repeated heads: the pitch alone gives the notehead, the same for every duration; a minim two heads with a stem, a semibreve four without, a breve two tied groups of four (today's repeat mode).
  1. Register.
  2. A design document in docs/planning/ with mock-ups the operator looks at before approving it: the Noteheads step opening with the choice of design, and for B the way duration shows; what the notehead panel offers under each (no Fill under A and B.i; the colour alone, and the height, under A); what becomes of the duration buttons, the Shape or Repeat choice and the Hollow minims tick above the canvas; where the conventional noteheads come from (the operator will draw them in SVG if the app needs them); how the choice is stored and exported (today's durations.representationMode and rules where they suffice); and how a saved notation that fits none of the three opens (several gallery files vary fill by pitch with hollow minims), as the key signature's starting points show "your own symbols".
  3. The engine session's brief for B.i's breve, in docs/planning/: in shape mode, a breve drawn as a double notehead or as two tied semibreves, the designer's choice, and the figures the engine is to measure, as session 72's brief did. The engine session runs after this one, released as an engine minor version.
