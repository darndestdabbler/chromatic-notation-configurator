Session 34: The preview draws what the engine engraves

Why. Session 33 found the preview and the engraving differ wherever the preview has a setting the engine never receives, and the operator's rule is that a difference a user could notice, or that would make them doubt the export, is fixed rather than documented. Measured on 2026-11-07 against the engine, default config, in staff spaces: stems are 0.13 thick and reach about 3.5 past the far head's centre on the page, but 0.065 and 1.70 in the preview; staff lines are 0.10 on the page and 0.05 in the preview. Staff and ledger line thickness, colour and dash, ledger width and rounded ends, the ledger rules, key-scoped notehead rules, the repeat counts and the key-signature gutter all change the preview and nothing on the page (LY spec §5.2a). The operator chose, per gap (2026-11-07):

  • where the engine has its own fixed value and no setting in its simple mode, the preview draws the engine's value and the setting leaves the editors. The engine's advanced line mode (cnb:staff-and-ledger-lines) is not used;
  • ledger rules are exported: the engine's simple mode takes, for each notehead position, the exact ledgers to draw (cnb:ledger-overrides, cnb/ledgers.ly), so the ledger editor keeps working;
  • the three gaps only the engine can close go to the engine repository's plan, as its session 24.
  1. Register.
  2. Stems. The preview draws a stem at the engine's thickness and length, in staff spaces (axis units per staff space under a pitch axis): LilyPond's 0.13 thickness and its length past the far notehead, including the cases where LilyPond shortens a stem or carries it to the middle line, as measured against the engine. display.stemThicknessPct and display.stemExtension stop being read. An engine-backed test in cnb/tests/cnb-card-tests.js pins stem thickness and both ends, crotchet and minim, up and down, for the default and Kite fixtures.
  3. Staff and ledger lines. The preview draws staff lines and ledgers at the engine's thickness and length, black and solid, measured from a render. The line settings the engine does not take (staff and ledger thickness, colour, dash, line overrides, ledger width multiplier and rounded ends) leave the staff editor and stop being read; configs that carry them still load. An engine-backed test pins the measured sizes.
  4. Which ledgers appear. The export writes, for every notehead position in the engine's range, the ledgers the preview draws for a note there, as cnb:ledger-overrides (and cnb:ledger-positions if the overrides need them), linear and pitch-axis alike, so the engraving shows the preview's ledgers, in-gap ledgers included. An engine round trip compares the engraved ledger positions with the preview's for the default, Kite 4-369, 45deg-369 and Twinline fixtures and every gallery file. This retires the "in-gap ledger difference" listed under "Limits kept on purpose".
  5. Key-scoped notehead rules. Section C dispatches on the key the engine passes (the key's pitch-class list), so a rule whose match.keys names a key definition engraves as the preview draws it. Validation stops warning that such rules are preview-only. An engine round trip checks one key-scoped colour rule in two keys.
  6. The rest. The key-signature gutter follows the engine's spacing and its setting leaves the editor; the repeat counts leave the editor (the engine's counts are fixed, session 18). The baselines (export and preview) are regenerated and every change is listed.
  7. LY spec 1.3.9 records the ledger and key exports and §5.2a's shorter preview-only list; the architecture plan's display, staff and ledger sections say what the preview now draws. The README's "Preview-only settings" list shrinks to what remains (the colour-blind palette toggle, which the score selects).
